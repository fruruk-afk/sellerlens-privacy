ArtList Helper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

ArtList Helper ("the extension") helps art and print-on-demand sellers write better product listings faster. This policy explains exactly what the extension does and does not do with your data. In short: the extension runs locally in your browser and does not collect, sell, or transmit your personal data to us.

What we collect

Nothing. ArtList Helper has no account, no login, and no analytics or tracking. We (the developer) do not receive, store, or have access to any of your data.

Where your data is stored

Your settings, saved templates, and generation history are stored only on your own device using the browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly use the optional AI feature described below. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its storage.

Pages the extension reads

When you are on a supported marketplace page (e.g. Etsy) and choose to use a feature, the extension reads only the content already visible on that page (such as search-results signals) to give you suggestions. It reads on demand, does not run background scraping loops, and never edits, posts, or publishes anything on your behalf. You review and publish every listing yourself.

Optional AI generation (Pro feature — off by default)

The free version generates titles, tags, and descriptions using a built-in, offline, rule-based generator — no data leaves your device.

The Pro version can optionally use an external AI provider (for example OpenAI or Anthropic) to generate higher-quality wording. This feature is opt-in. If you enable it, you supply your own API key, which is stored locally on your device. When you generate with AI, the text you provide (such as your product type, style, and keywords) is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose, under your own account and their privacy policy. ArtList Helper does not route this through our servers and does not receive a copy.

What we never do

We never sell data, never show ads, never automate marketplace actions on your behalf, and never access your marketplace account credentials.

Not affiliated

ArtList Helper is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Etsy, Redbubble, or any marketplace or AI provider. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.
